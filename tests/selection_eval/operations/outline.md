# Northgate Logistics: Restoring On-Time Delivery

Audience: Northgate operations leadership team. Purpose: explain why on-time delivery fell, what it costs, and which fixes to fund.

Slide 1: Title Slide
Title: Restoring On-Time Delivery
Subtitle: Diagnosis and improvement plan for the Northgate warehouse network

Slide 2: On-Time Delivery Has Missed Target for Five Quarters
On-time delivery (OTD), % of orders, against a 95% target:

| Quarter | Q3 2023 | Q4 2023 | Q1 2024 | Q2 2024 | Q3 2024 | Q4 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OTD % | 95.4 | 93.1 | 91.8 | 90.2 | 89.5 | 90.7 |

Slide 3: Four Groups of Causes Drive Late Deliveries
Root-cause workshop with site managers. The late-delivery problem is caused by:
- People: high agency-staff turnover; pickers untrained on the new scanners
- Process: order cut-off times not aligned with carrier pick-ups; manual exception handling
- Systems: warehouse system releases waves late; no real-time carrier tracking
- Equipment: two of six conveyor lines down more than 10% of shifts

Slide 4: Late Deliveries Added 65 Cents to the Cost of Every Order
Cost per order rose from USD 4.20 in 2023 to USD 4.85 in 2024: overtime +0.32, re-deliveries +0.21, fuel +0.17, partly offset by automation savings of −0.05.

Slide 5: A Late Order Passes Through Four Teams Before It Reaches the Customer
Exception process today:
- Customer service logs the late order and promises a new date
- Warehouse re-picks the order and books a new carrier slot
- Carrier collects the next day and updates tracking
- Finance issues a delivery-fee refund to the customer
Each hand-off waits for an email; no team sees the whole order.

Slide 9: Two Initiatives Offer High Impact for Low Effort
Six initiatives scored for impact (1–10) and effort (1–10):
- Align cut-off times with carrier slots: impact 8, effort 2
- Scanner training for all pickers: impact 7, effort 3
- Real-time carrier tracking: impact 8, effort 7
- Replace conveyor lines: impact 6, effort 9
- Retention bonus for agency staff: impact 5, effort 4
- Automated exception workflow: impact 7, effort 6

Slide 11: Decisions Needed From Leadership
- Approve USD 1.2M for real-time carrier tracking, so customer service can promise reliable dates
- Mandate aligned cut-off times across all sites from March, overriding local carrier contracts
- Extend the scanner-training pilot to Riverside and Lakeside, funded from the overtime budget
