Slide 5: Trial Is the Bottleneck in the Purchase Funnel
Thailand consumer survey (n = 2,000 urban adults), share of respondents at each stage: aware of oat milk 100%, considered buying 62%, tried at least once 31%, buy monthly 14%. The biggest drop is from consideration to trial.

Slide 6: Most Volume Will Flow Through Distributors Into Modern Trade
Planned 2027 volume of 12,000 tonnes: 7,200 t through national distributors and 4,800 t direct. Distributors deliver 4,300 t to modern trade, 1,900 t to foodservice and 1,000 t to traditional trade. Direct volume goes 3,600 t to e-commerce and 1,200 t to key-account cafés.

Slide 7: Shoppers Drop Off at the Shelf and After the First Carton
Consumer journey from interviews:
- Discover: social videos and café menus spark interest — curious
- Compare: unclear nutrition claims on pack — confused
- Buy: product often out of stock in convenience stores — frustrated
- Use: taste in coffee rated highly — satisfied
- Repurchase: large carton too heavy for scooter delivery — annoyed

Slide 9: Supply and Regulatory Risks Need Owners Before Launch
Five risks rated for probability and impact:
- Oat supply disruption: probability medium, impact high
- Halal certification delay (Indonesia, Malaysia): probability high, impact medium
- Price war by Oatside: probability high, impact high
- JV partner misalignment: probability low, impact high
- Cold-chain cost overrun: probability medium, impact medium

Slide 10: Five Factors Decide Success in the Region
- Barista partnerships that put the product in front of first-time drinkers
- Smaller pack sizes for scooter delivery and convenience stores
- Local-language nutrition claims that pass regulator review
- Halal certification before launch in Indonesia and Malaysia
- A distributor incentive scheme tied to on-shelf availability

Slide 11: A Three-Phase Entry Over 18 Months
- Phase 1 Pilot (Jan–Jun 2026): Bangkok cafés and e-commerce; milestone: 300 café accounts
- Phase 2 Scale (Jul 2026–Mar 2027): JV production starts; national modern trade in Thailand and Vietnam; milestone: 5,000 stores
- Phase 3 Expand (Apr–Jun 2027): Indonesia and Malaysia after halal approval; milestone: four-country presence
