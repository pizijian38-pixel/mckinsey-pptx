# Lumen Apparel: Q4 2024 Business Review

Audience: Lumen Apparel leadership team, quarterly business review.

Slide 1: Title Slide
Title: Q4 2024 Business Review
Subtitle: Lumen Apparel leadership team

Slide 2: Revenue Grew in Every Region, Fastest in the South
Revenue by region (USD million):

| Region | Q1 2024 | Q2 2024 | Q3 2024 | Q4 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| North | 42 | 44 | 45 | 48 |
| South | 28 | 31 | 35 | 40 |
| East | 35 | 35 | 36 | 38 |
| West | 22 | 23 | 23 | 25 |

Slide 3: Outerwear Is Now Two-Fifths of Revenue
Q4 2024 revenue mix by category: outerwear 40%, knitwear 27%, denim 21%, accessories 12%.

Slide 4: Gross Margin Fell 2.4 Points Over the Year
Gross margin went from 41.0% in Q1 to 38.6% in Q4: freight −1.4 points, discounting −1.6 points, partly offset by a better category mix +0.6 points.

Slide 5: Only the South Beat Its Revenue Target
Q4 2024 revenue against target (USD million): North 48 vs 51, South 40 vs 37, East 38 vs 42, West 25 vs 27.

Slide 6: Store Managers Raised Five Issues
Feedback from the store-manager forum:
- Staffing: weekend rosters short by two people on average
- Stock-outs: core sizes in denim missing for up to ten days
- App returns: online returns processed in store take 15 minutes each
- Fitting rooms: queues of more than ten minutes on Saturdays
- Payments: card terminals fail during peak hours

Slide 7: How Do We Restore Gross Margin to 41%?
The margin gap can be closed through three levers:
- Reduce freight cost: consolidate shipments; renegotiate the sea-freight contract
- Reduce discounting: fewer promotional weeks; clear excess stock through outlets
- Improve mix: grow outerwear and accessories; raise knitwear prices
